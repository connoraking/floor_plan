Quick help
  • Click a page before calibrating or adding furniture.
  • Each page has its own Remove button. At least one page is always kept.
  • Drag furniture to move it. Arrow keys make small adjustments.
  • Use the Zoom slider, or hold Ctrl while scrolling over a page.
  • Export PDF makes a shareable PDF with the furniture visible. Save editable keeps a project you can change later.
  • Use Delete to remove the selected piece.
